					Simulated
					
    Stood in for the real thing.
    
					
    Nothing is issued, and nothing leaves your browser.
    
				
    
				
      
					
    • 01
      GLEIF and the QVI
      The root, GEDA and QVI identifiers are generated here. They are not GLEIF's or any QVI's real AIDs.
      • 
					
    • 02
      Digests
      SAIDs use SHA2-256 (CESR code I) where production uses Blake3-256 (code E). ACDC SAIDs are taken over the compact form.
      • 
					
    • 03
      Infrastructure
      No witnesses, receipts, watchers or OOBI discovery. The IPEX exchange is collapsed into one signed message.

      Identity assurance
      The checks a QVI makes on representatives and role holders before issuing are skipped entirely.
