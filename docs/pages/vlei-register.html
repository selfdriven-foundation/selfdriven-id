The premise

Three routes to an LEI, one to a vLEI.

An LEI comes from an accredited LEI issuer, either directly or through a registration agent or a validation agent. Every route ends with the same code in GLEIF's index.

A vLEI comes only from a Qualified vLEI Issuer, and only once the organisation holds an LEI. The QVI checks the people who will act for it before issuing anything.

Choose on price and service. The code is the same.

See the four steps

Issuer direct

Apply on an accredited issuer's site. It checks your details against the official register and publishes the LEI to GLEIF's index.

  • ~40 issuers
  • Often same day
  • Renew yearly

Through an agent

Registration agents apply on your behalf through a partner issuer, often for less. Validation agents, usually banks, obtain the LEI while onboarding you.

  • 100+ agents
  • Validation agents
  • Same code

vLEI from a QVI

Once the LEI is issued, contract a Qualified vLEI Issuer. It verifies your representatives, then issues the Legal Entity vLEI and the role credentials you authorise.

  • 9 QVIs
  • LEI first
  • KERI wallet

The steps

Four steps, two credentials.

Most of the effort is the first application. After that it is upkeep: renew the LEI every year, and keep the vLEI under your QVI's maintenance terms.

  1. 01 · LEI

    Apply online

    Pick an issuer or agent and enter your registration number; most services pull the rest from the register. Confirm addresses and parents, then pay.

    Compare LEI services
  2. 02 · LEI

    Validate and publish

    The issuer checks your data against the register and publishes the LEI to GLEIF's index, often the same day. Renew every year to keep it ISSUED.

    What you'll need
  3. 03 · vLEI

    Contract a QVI

    A designated authorised representative signs up with a QVI and names the legal entity authorised representatives who will approve credentials.

    Compare QVIs
  4. 04 · vLEI

    Verify and issue

    The QVI verifies those people, often by live video, then issues the Legal Entity vLEI to your KERI identifier, and OOR or ECR credentials as you authorise them.

Agents

Registration and validation agents.

Agents don't issue LEIs: an accredited issuer behind them does, and stays responsible to GLEIF.

  • Registration agent · AULEI Register (LEI Code Australia)Works with RapidLEI and Nasdaq LEI. Asks for an ACN, or an ABN for a trust; LEIs usually within 24 hours.
  • GLEIFAll registration agentsMore than 100, listed under each issuer in GLEIF's search tool.
  • Validation agent · AUCommonwealth BankA GLEIF validation agent since February 2024: it can obtain an LEI for clients as part of onboarding.
  • Before you pay

    Check the agent

    Agents must name their partner issuer and show issuance, renewal and transfer services separately, under GLEIF's governance framework.

Checklist

What you'll need.

Have these ready before you start any application.

  • 01

    Registration number

    An ACN for a company, or an ABN for a trust or trustee, checked against the official register.

  • 02

    Addresses

    The legal address and the headquarters address, as the register shows them.

  • 03

    Parents

    Direct and ultimate accounting parents, or the reason none is reported, for GLEIF's Level 2 data.

  • 04

    Authority

    Evidence you may apply for the entity. Trusts usually need the trust deed as well.

vLEI services

Where to get a vLEI.

Only GLEIF's Qualified vLEI Issuers can issue one, and each sets its own fees. None is based in Australia yet, so check that your chosen QVI serves your jurisdiction.

The process

What a QVI will ask for.

The shape is the same at every QVI; the forms and fees differ.

  • 01

    An issued LEI

    The organisation needs its LEI first. The Legal Entity vLEI carries it.

  • 02

    Representatives

    A designated authorised representative (DAR) signs up and names the legal entity authorised representatives (LARs) who approve credentials.

  • 03

    Identity checks

    The QVI verifies each representative and role holder. Depending on the QVI, that can mean a live video call.

  • 04

    A KERI wallet

    Credentials go to identifiers the organisation controls. Some QVIs, Provenant among them, host the wallet for you.

Local context

Where the LEI already matters.

Derivatives reporting made it compulsory for some; banks are starting to ask for it more widely.

  • Required

    OTC derivatives

    Since 21 October 2024, entities trading OTC derivatives have needed an LEI under ASIC's Derivative Transaction Rules (Reporting) 2024.

  • Bank route

    Ask your bank

    Commonwealth Bank is a GLEIF validation agent and can obtain an LEI for clients during onboarding.

  • The record

    Validated at ASIC

    Company LEIs are checked against ASIC's Register of Companies, as selfdriven Pty Ltd's was.

  • vLEI

    Issued from offshore

    No QVI is based in Australia yet. Sydney's Bloom Fintech reported taking its vLEI through TradeGo in Singapore in September 2026.
