The premise

A shared identification system comes first. Identity and trust follow from it.

Without a shared identification system, you can't have shareable identity. Without shareable identities, you can't have shared trust. selfdriven.id works from that foundation up — portable identifiers, verifiable credentials, and the trust layer underneath.

Identification → identity → trust.

What this covers

Identification

Who is acting, proven rather than assumed. Self-certifying KERI identifiers (AIDs) derived directly from keys, with witnessed key events under every identity.

  • AID
  • DID
  • WebAuthn

Verifiable data

What can be proven. ACDC credentials and attestations chained to the KERI trust layer, with selective disclosure and issuer-independent revocation.

  • ACDC
  • VC
  • vLEI

Trust & governance

How authority is held and delegated. Key rotation, delegation, and revocation give humans and AI agents scoped, revocable authority rooted in a durable identity.

  • KEL
  • Delegation
  • Rotation
